# Oasis and your privacy (for students)

Oasis is what your teacher uses to give you video lessons with questions. Your school said it's OK for you to use it. Here's what it knows about you and what it does with that.

**What Oasis knows about you**

- Your name and school email, from Google and your teacher's Google Classroom class.
- Which lessons you've watched, how far you got, and when.
- Your answers and scores.
- When you leave fullscreen, switch tabs, or click away during a lesson.
- How often you open Oasis and roughly how long it's open.
- Anything you write in a bug report.

**What Oasis can't see:** your other tabs, your screen, your camera, your microphone, where you are, or anything you do outside Oasis. It never sees your Google password.

**Who can see your stuff**

- **Your teacher** sees your progress, answers, scores, and how many times you left the lesson.
- **Other students** can't see any of it.
- **The people who run Oasis** can reach it if they need to fix a problem or keep Oasis safe. They don't browse it for fun.
- **AI grading:** if your teacher turns it on, your short answer (but not your name or email) may be sent to an AI service to suggest a grade. Your teacher has the final say.

**What we never do:** sell your information, show you ads, or use your information for anything except your class.

**Leaving a lesson isn't automatically cheating.** Oasis just tells your teacher it happened. If something glitched, tell your teacher.

**Want to see or delete your information?** Ask your teacher or a parent to contact your school, or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
