## 1. What Oasis is

Oasis lets teachers add questions to YouTube videos, assign them through Google Classroom, and see how their students did. It is currently a **free pilot** run by {{OPERATOR}}. Features may change, and things may break. We'll tell schools ahead of time about big changes.

## 2. Accounts

- You sign in with a Google account. Use your school Google account.
- Teacher accounts are for teachers and school staff. Getting a teacher account with false information, or helping a student get one, is not allowed: teacher accounts can see answer keys.
- Student accounts come from a teacher's Google Classroom roster. Students can't create classes or join classes on their own.
- Keep your Google account secure. You're responsible for what happens under your account. Tell us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} if you think someone else is using it.

## 3. Schools and teachers

If you use Oasis with students, you confirm that you're allowed to by your school, and that your school is OK with Oasis's [Privacy Policy](/privacy). Schools that want a signed data privacy agreement can see [For schools](/schools).

Your lessons are yours. You give us permission to store and show them in Oasis so Oasis works (for example, showing your questions to your students, or to other teachers if you choose to share a lesson). You're responsible for what you add: only use videos and content you're allowed to use, and follow [YouTube's Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

**Grades are the teacher's call.** Oasis grades multiple-choice answers automatically and, if you turn it on, suggests grades for short answers with AI. Review grades before you rely on them or send them to Google Classroom. AI suggestions can be wrong.

**Anti-cheat is a signal, not proof.** Oasis stops skipping ahead in a video and records when a student leaves fullscreen or switches tabs. It can't see what students do outside the lesson, and a focus event can have an innocent cause (a pop-up, a slow Chromebook, a notification). Don't discipline a student based on Oasis alone.

## 4. Rules for everyone

Don't:

- Try to see answers, grades, or information you aren't supposed to see, or get around anti-skip or anti-cheat features with scripts, tools, or by changing how Oasis works.
- Break into, overload, scan, or attack Oasis, or use bots or scrapers.
- Post anything illegal, hateful, harassing, or sexual, or anyone else's private information.
- Pretend to be someone else.

If you find a security problem, please tell us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} instead of using it. We appreciate it, and we won't take action against good-faith reports.

We may suspend accounts that break these rules. For a student, we'll tell the teacher or school rather than acting on our own, unless it's needed to stop harm right away.
